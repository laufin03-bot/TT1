Privacy

Your budget belongs to you. My Budget saves everything you enter in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, plus a copy on your device so the app works offline.

What the app can access

When you sign in, you let My Budget:

  • create and open the one spreadsheet it makes for you (it cannot see any other files in your Google Drive), and
  • see your email address, so it can show which Google account you're signed in with.

The app never reads your email messages, contacts, or other Google data.

What we store

Nothing. There is no My Budget server and no database. Your information goes directly between your device and Google. We never see it.

Removing your data

Delete the "My Budget" spreadsheet from your Google Drive, and use "Sign out on this device" in the app's Settings. To remove the app's access to your Google account completely, visit myaccount.google.com/connections.
